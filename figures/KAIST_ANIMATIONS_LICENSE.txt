KAIST controller replay animations and preview images

Files: kaist_controller_detections.gif/.png and
kaist_controller_night_detections.gif/.png.

The underlying RGB/thermal imagery is from the KAIST Multispectral Pedestrian
Detection Benchmark, Soonmin Hwang, Jaesik Park, Namil Kim, Yukyung Choi and
In So Kweon, CVPR 2015. Official release:
https://github.com/SoonminHwang/rgbt-ped-detection

The dataset-derived animations and previews are provided under Creative Commons
Attribution NonCommercial ShareAlike 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Modifications: paired views, saved model detections, controller resolution trace,
measurement overlays, temporal sampling and GIF encoding. The JSON sidecars
identify source runs, frames, thresholds and the illustrative selection process.
